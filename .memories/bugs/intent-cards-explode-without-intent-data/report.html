The headline

The grouping code was never the main problem — there was no intent data anywhere on your machine. The hooks installed in ~/.claude/settings.json were hard-pinned to ...\Code\ONEflight, so every edit made in any other repo was silently discarded. No edits.jsonl had ever been written; ONEflight had 52 orphaned labels with nothing to join to. With zero records, attribution abstains on every hunk and the last-resort "enclosing symbol" pass makes roughly one card per hunk — your 133 groups. On top of that, the fallback itself had title bugs ("New usize" named the type, not the variable; the NEW badge duplicated the "New" in the label).

What to expect now

  • Imported-history titles are coarse by design (nearest prose sentence, e.g. "The plan is approved"). Titles get sharp from here on: the CLAUDE.md instruction asks agents to end each editing turn with Intent: <3-5 words>.
  • Hunks no agent explains still get conservative location cards ("a wrong label is much worse than no label") — but now one per file, not one per hunk, and the panel tells you when data is missing instead of degrading silently.
  • All changes are uncommitted on claude/lightweight-ide-replacement-52cp2n, ready for your review.
